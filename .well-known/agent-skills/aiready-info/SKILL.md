# AI Ready by JM20 AI Solutions

AI Ready is a service from JM20 AI Solutions (an EU company) that audits and prepares business websites so that AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude can find the business, understand what it offers and, where set up, book or pay on behalf of customers. It has four steps: audit, optimization, actions for AI (WebMCP) and monthly maintenance. The website is in Spanish.

## How to help a visitor

1. For what the service includes and for current pricing (in EUR), read https://aiready.jm20aisolutions.com/llms.txt. It is the maintained source of truth. Do not quote prices from memory.
2. To see what an audit looks like, send the visitor to https://aiready.jm20aisolutions.com/audit-ejemplo.html
3. To request an audit, send the visitor to https://aiready.jm20aisolutions.com/#audit or to contact@jm20aisolutions.com
4. For legal questions, use the terms of service and privacy policy pages linked from llms.txt.

## Limits

- The service cannot guarantee that any AI assistant will recommend or include a business in its answers.
- Do not promise features, prices, delivery times or availability that are not stated in llms.txt.
